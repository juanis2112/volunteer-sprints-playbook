# Hub join and next steps

Gives accepted participants step-by-step instructions for joining the community hub and channel, plus a pre-kickoff checklist.

**When:** T-4w (around [Confirm-by date]); resend with a fresh invite link if the original expires.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with mentors and Team Leads copied.

**Audience:** All accepted participants (one email, all recipients in BCC).

## Template

```text
Subject: Join the community hub + what we need from you before [Start date]

Hi!

Two things in this one: how to join the community hub, and a few next steps before kickoff.

1. Join the community hub 💬 (required)
The hub is where the series lives between office hours. It's where you'll meet mentors across all the projects, connect with participants from [Partner companies], get announcements, find notes and resources, and ask the questions you'd rather not raise on a big call. Please do it today!

There are two steps, and both matter. Joining the workspace does not put you in our channel.

Step 1: Join the workspace
- Open this invite link: [Hub invite link]
- Enter your personal email address when prompted
- Enter the confirmation code from your inbox
- Set your display name and finish setup
[If your company restricts external workspaces on work devices: - Join on a personal device first, then add the workspace in the app on your work computer: "Sign in to another workspace" and enter [Hub workspace URL].]

Step 2: Ask us to add you to the channel ⚠️
Once you're in the workspace, message [Team Lead name] or [Program organizer name] and we'll add you to [Hub channel]. This is the step people forget. Without it you're in the workspace but invisible to the cohort.

Then introduce yourself in [Hub channel]: who you are and what you'd like to work on (docs, testing, performance, triage, reviews, anything). We use intros to connect you with mentors and participants working on similar things.

Things that trip people up
- Use your personal email, not your work email. This is an external community workspace.
- If you keep getting redirected to your company's sign-in, you're being routed to your work workspace. Re-enter [Hub workspace URL].
- If your browser signs you in as the wrong account, use a private or incognito window.
- No code? Check spam. Codes expire fast, so request a new one rather than reusing an old one.
- Joined in the browser but it doesn't show in the app? Fully quit and relaunch.

2. Confirm your spot ✅ (only if you haven't already)
If you haven't confirmed yet, reply by [Confirm-by date]. We have a waitlist of people hoping for a seat and will start offering places after that.

3. Your other next steps 📋
- Read the project guide for both your primary and secondary projects: [Project guide link]
- Read the collaboration guidance (chat and video setup, community norms, AI use): [Collaboration guidance link]
- Set up your development environment for both projects before [Start date], so week one isn't spent installing things: [Setup guide link]
- Send us your public github.com username if you haven't already
[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: - File [Approval process] by [Date]. It routes to your manager for approval.]
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: - Set up hour tracking through [Hour tracking process].]

4. Mark your calendar 📅
We kick off [Start date] at [Office hours day and time, with UTC], and office hours run at that time through [End date]. Zoom: [Zoom link]

Mentors and Team Leads are copied here and can help with anything: hub access, setup problems, project questions. That's what this support team is for. 🙌

Welcome again!

[Program organizer name]
```

## How to use

- Test the invite link the day you send; many invite links expire after a set time or number of uses. Prefer a non-expiring link.
- Walk through the join steps yourself on a work device to confirm they match your company's setup, and adjust the troubleshooting list.
- Delete the confirmation section if everyone has already confirmed.
- Track who has joined [Hub channel] and follow up individually with anyone missing a week before kickoff.

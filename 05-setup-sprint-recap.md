# Setup Sprint recap

Thanks Setup Sprint attendees, summarizes what they validated, and points them to the series.

**When:** Within a week of the last sprint session (about T-11w).
**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer (with any co-host)
**Audience:** Everyone who attended a Setup Sprint

## Template

```text
Subject: Thank you: Setup Sprint recap and what's next

Hi everyone,

Thank you for showing up, diving in, and making a real difference at the Setup Sprint[s]. [If co-hosted: This was a first collaboration between [Co-host] and [Company]'s open source team, and it worked because of you.]

What you accomplished
Across [N] session[s], you tested and documented developer environment setup for [N] projects. Status by project:

- [Project]: [Environment 1]: working. [Environment 2]: working. [Environment 3]: not yet tested.
- [Project]: [Environment 1]: working. [Environment 2]: working with workaround.
- [Project]: ...

You also recorded [N] fixes and workarounds in the setup guides. This is the onboarding foundation for everyone joining the series.

What's next: the Sustaining Open Source Series
From [Start date] to [End date], participants from [Company] [and [Partner companies]] will contribute to these same projects over 10 weeks, working with maintainers on real issues and in the project communities. The guides you built are where they will start.

How to stay involved
- Apply to participate: applications open [Date] at [Form link] (deadline [Application deadline]).
- Interested in mentoring? Reply to this email.
- Join [Open source community channel] for updates and the series kickoff.

Thank you again. This community is something special.

[Program organizer name]
[If co-hosted: on behalf of [Co-host] and [Company]]
```

## How to use

- Fill in the per-project status from the groups' notes. Only mark "working" where someone confirmed tests run.
- List any environment that is still untested so mentors know where to expect trouble.
- Send before applications open so attendees can apply first.
- Do not include headcount or hour totals unless you have verified them from your sign-up or volunteer platform.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The structure is universal; co-host acknowledgement and environments are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** co-host name, environments tested (managed laptops, internal cloud dev environments), community channel name.
- **Derived from:** "[Comms] PRIDE Sustainability Series Sprint" (post-sprint email).

# Weekly Pulse Poll

A bank of quick one-question polls to post after office hours, so organizers can spot blockers early without a full survey.

**When:** Weekly, the day after each session (T+1d to T+9w). Use one poll per week.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** All participants in [Hub channel].

## Template

### Where are you in your journey?

```text
🎉 Thanks to everyone who joined office hours! Quick check-in: where are you in your [Project] journey? ✨

🛠️ Setup Mode: still getting my environment working
🧭 Explorer Mode: reading the codebase, docs, and issues
🎯 Quest Accepted: I've claimed an issue or started on something
🚀 In Review: I have a PR open or I'm reviewing someone else's
🤝 Regular: I'm showing up in project channels or community meetings
🎉 Merged: I've had a contribution accepted
```

### What's blocking you most?

```text
What's the biggest thing in your way right now? 🚧

⏰ Finding the time
🔍 Finding the right issue
🧠 Understanding the codebase
🛠️ Dev environment setup
🔁 Waiting on review
🤷 Nothing, I'm cruising!
```

### What would help most next week?

```text
What would help you most next week? 💡

🗺️ Live help finding an issue
👀 Someone to look at my PR
🧪 A walkthrough of tests or CI
🤝 A partner to work with
💬 Knowing which community meeting to join
```

### How did today's session land?

```text
How useful was today's session? 🙌

🔥 Super useful
👍 Useful
😐 It was OK
🔄 Not for me this week (tell us why in a thread!)
```

## How to use

- One poll per week. Rotate based on what you need to learn; the journey poll works well every two to three weeks to show movement.
- Results are self-reported and stay internal. Use them to steer the next session, not as outcome metrics.
- Post a short "here's what we heard and what we're changing" note the following week, so people see their answers matter.
- If many people pick "Finding the right issue," ask the issue-list owner to refresh the list before the next session.

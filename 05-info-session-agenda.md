# Info session agenda

A 60-minute run of show for the info session held while applications are open.

**When:** T-8w for the first session; optional second session at T-7w, at least a week before [Application deadline]. Share the invite with the announcement at T-10w.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer runs the session; Team Leads, Company mentors, Project mentors, and alumni speak.

**Audience:** Prospective participants and mentors (invite managers too). Speakers get the run of show; attendees get the short agenda.

## Template

### Run of show (internal, for speakers)

- **0:00-0:03 Welcome** (Program organizer)
  - Purpose of the session, how Q&A works (questions in chat or a shared doc), recording yes or no
  - Ask attendees to add their name to the attendance list
- **0:03-0:10 What the series is and what's new this round** (Team Lead)
  - Projects in [Project list] and [Foundation]
  - Dates: [Start date] to [End date], office hours [Office hours day and time, with UTC]
  - Partners: [Partner companies]
  - What's new: e.g. new projects, mini-cohorts, a regional cohort, a Setup Sprint
- **0:10-0:20 Alumni panel: what it was actually like** (two or three past participants)
  - What you worked on, how it worked week to week, what surprised you, what you got out of it
  - Keep it informal; the moderator asks one or two prompts each
  - [Optional: link a public write-up or blog post from a previous series]
- **0:20-0:35 Meet the leads and mentors** (Team Leads, Company mentors, Project mentors)
  - Each speaker: who they are, which project, what they're excited about, their hopes for the cohort (one to two minutes each)
- **0:35-0:42 What contributing looks like** (Company mentor or Project mentor)
  - Code, docs, tests, reviews, triage, community meetings, answering questions all count
  - Mini-cohorts and weekly check-ins; how primary and secondary projects split
  - Realistic time commitment: about [N] hours a week
  - [If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: how hours are tracked and matched]
  - [If your company requires internal approval to contribute: the approval step and the note we send managers]
- **0:42-0:45 How to apply and how selection works** (Program organizer)
  - Form: [Form link]; deadline: [Application deadline]
  - Cohort size ([N]) and decision date ([Decision date])
  - How we select: real use and a specific target, open source experience or excitement, effort in answers, commitment, project balance by mentor capacity
- **0:45-0:58 Open Q&A** (all)
  - Seed questions if the room is quiet (see below)
- **0:58-1:00 Close** (Program organizer)
  - Recap the deadline and link, where the recording and FAQ will be posted, thank speakers

### Seed questions for Q&A

- How much time per week did the series realistically take?
- Do I need to be an expert in [Domain] to contribute?
- What if my manager has questions about the time commitment?
- How does the time count toward [Matching program name]? (Only if your company has one.)
- What are the selection criteria?
- How is time split between the primary and secondary project?
- What if I'm in a different time zone?

### Short agenda (for the calendar invite)

```text
Sustaining Open Source Series: Info Session
[Info session date and time] | [Zoom link]

Thinking about applying? Come hear what the series is like, meet the mentors, and ask anything.

- Welcome and what's new this round
- Alumni panel: what it was actually like
- Meet the leads and mentors
- What contributing looks like
- How to apply and how selection works
- Open Q&A

Apply by [Application deadline]: [Form link]
Add your questions ahead of time: [Shared doc link]
```

## How to use

- Confirm speakers and timing a week ahead; send speakers the run of show and keep each mentor intro to two minutes.
- Open a shared doc for attendance and questions, and paste the seed questions in so people can add to them.
- Decide on recording in advance and say so at the start; post the recording and the FAQ link afterwards.
- If attendees span time zones, run a second session at a time that works for the other region.
- Add any new recurring questions to the FAQ (04-participant-faq.md).

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. The structure works for any company; only the speakers and conditional segments change.
- **Company-specific parts:** speakers, matching and approval segments, links, whether to fold the session into an existing open source office hours slot.
- **Derived from:** NumFOCUS series info session agenda (Open Source Office Hours agenda doc: welcome, what's new, alumni panel, meet leads and mentors, how to apply, open Q&A with seeded questions); Juanita's playbook (Stage 2, Task 1, item 2: host at least one info session, aim for two).

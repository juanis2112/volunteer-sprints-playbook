# Keep Contributing Message

A post-series message that shows participants how to stay engaged with their project after the 10 weeks end.

**When:** T+9w to T+10w (at the celebration session, then posted in the channel and emailed).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with Team Leads adding company-specific lines.

**Audience:** All participants.

## Template

```text
Subject: The series is over. Your open source journey isn't! 🚀

Hi [Participant first name],

Thank you for being part of the Sustaining Open Source Series. Whether you merged a PR, reviewed one, joined a community meeting, or spent ten weeks learning how a project really works, you're now part of [Project]'s community. Here's how to keep showing up.

Stay connected to your project
- Community and working group meetings: [Project] meets [frequency]. Calendar: [Link]
- Project channel: [Project channel]. Keep asking and answering questions there
- Mailing list or forum: [Link]

Ways to keep contributing (pick what fits your time)
- Triage: reproduce bug reports, add labels or missing details, close duplicates
- Review: read open PRs and leave thoughtful comments or test them locally
- Docs: fix what confused you when you started
- Answer questions from newer contributors
- Pick up an issue: [Curated issue list or label link]
- Follow up on anything you left open: stalled PRs, claimed issues

Where this can lead
Many projects have a path from contributor to triager, reviewer, and maintainer. It comes from showing up consistently over time. Ask a maintainer what that path looks like in [Project]: [Governance or contributor ladder link]

[If your company has a program for continued contribution: To keep contributing as part of your work, use [Company program]. To contribute on personal time, use [Company program].]
[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: Contributions after the series may need [Approval process]. Check before you start.]
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: Keep logging volunteer hours through [Matching program name] so they can be matched to [Foundation].]

Give back to the next cohort
Interested in mentoring next time? Tell us here: [Form link]

And if you haven't yet, please fill out the end-of-series survey: [Form link]

We'll check in again in a few months to see how you're doing. Thank you for showing up!

[Program organizer name]
```

## How to use

- Fill in each project's real community meeting calendar, channel, and contributor ladder link. If a project has no written ladder, say "ask a maintainer" rather than inventing one.
- Each Team Lead adds or removes the company lines before sending to their own participants.
- Send it from the hub channel and by email, since some people stop checking the channel after the last session.
- Calendar a 3-month and 6-month follow-up now; they feed the retention metric in the outcomes summary.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The engagement guidance is universal; continuation programs, approvals, and matching are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** continued-contribution programs (work and personal time), approval steps, volunteer-hour matching and tracking.
- **Derived from:** Organizer Playbook Step 13 (continuation routes); Juanita's playbook Stage 5 Task 3 ("how to keep contributing after the sprint"); OTel Mid-Point Survey Report (requests for post-series continuity).

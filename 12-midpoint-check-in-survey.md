# Midpoint Check-In Survey

A short survey at the halfway mark to see who's progressing, what's blocking people, and what to change for the second half.

**When:** T+4w to T+5w. Keep it open for one week; share results at the next session.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** All participants.

## Template

Intro text:

```text
We're halfway through the Sustaining Open Source Series! 🎉 This 5-minute check-in helps us shape the second half. Your answers are used only by the organizers to improve the series.
```

Questions:

1. **Which project(s) are you contributing to?** (multiple choice: [Project list])
2. **How would you rate your progress so far?** (scale 1 to 10)
3. **What have you done so far?** (check all that apply)
   - Set up my dev environment
   - Read the codebase, docs, or issues
   - Joined a project channel or community meeting
   - Claimed or commented on an issue
   - Opened a pull request
   - Reviewed someone else's PR or helped triage
   - Had a contribution merged
   - Answered a question for another participant
4. **What's gotten in your way?** (check all that apply)
   - Finding the time
   - Finding or scoping the right issue
   - Understanding the codebase
   - Dev environment setup
   - The review process or waiting on review
   - Knowing who to ask
   - Nothing so far
5. **Have mentors helped?** (A lot / Somewhat / Not much / I haven't reached out yet)
6. **Which resources have been most valuable?** (check all that apply)
   - Office hours overview sessions
   - Breakout rooms
   - My mini-cohort lead's check-ins
   - Project channels
   - Curated issue list
   - Project guide and setup docs
   - Session notes and recordings
7. **Compared with Week 1, how confident do you feel contributing to open source?** (About the same / A bit more / Way more / I feel like a real contributor)
8. **Do you plan to keep contributing after the series?** (Yes / Probably / Not sure / No)
9. **What would help you keep going?** (check all that apply)
   - A curated issue list refreshed every week
   - Live help finding and scoping issues
   - Continued mentor access after the series
   - A working group or regular meetup
   - A partner to work with
   - Help finding the right community meeting or channel
10. **What's one thing that has helped most so far?** (open text)
11. **What's one thing we should change for the second half?** (open text)

## How to use

- Keep it to about 5 minutes. Drop questions you won't act on.
- Results are self-reported. Present them internally and to participants, labeled as self-reported, with the respondent count and date. Don't use intent questions (8) as external outcome metrics.
- Don't quote anyone by name without asking first.
- Close the loop: share 2 to 3 changes you're making at the next session.

# Waitlist email

Tells applicants they're on the waitlist, when they'll hear, and how to stay involved meanwhile.

**When:** T-5w, same day as acceptance emails.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Waitlisted applicants (one email, all recipients in BCC).

## Template

```text
Subject: You're on the waitlist: next steps for the Sustaining Open Source Series

Hi,

Thank you so much for applying to the Sustaining Open Source Series! We received [N] applications and have mentor capacity for [N] spots this round, so we can't bring everyone in right away. We're genuinely sorry about that.

The good news: you're on our waitlist. Spots often open up as people's plans change, and we'll let you know by [Waitlist decision date] if one opens for you.

In the meantime, you don't have to wait to get involved:
- Explore [Project list] and their contributing guides, and join a project's community chat.
[If your company has a general open source contribution program: - You can contribute to open source on your own through [Company program]. Details: [Program link]]
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: - Open source volunteer time may be eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

Stay connected
- [Company open source channel]
- [Company open source newsletter or mailing list]

We're grateful for your interest and can't wait to see how you contribute to open source, through the cohort or beyond.

Best,
The Sustaining Open Source Series team
```

## How to use

- Put all recipients in BCC; address the email to yourself.
- Fill in real counts only if you're comfortable sharing them; otherwise delete the numbers sentence and keep the capacity point.
- Keep the waitlist ranked per project, so you can offer spots that match the open seat's project.
- Set [Waitlist decision date] a few days before [Start date], and make sure someone sends final outcomes on that date.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The message is shared; the "meanwhile" options are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** general open source contribution program, matching program, internal open source channel and newsletter.

# AI Use Reminder

A friendly, private reminder for a participant whose contribution looks AI-generated without disclosure, plus a version for the whole channel.

**When:** As needed, as soon as a mentor or maintainer flags it.

**Sent by / owner:** Company mentor or Team Lead (private message). Program organizer (channel version).

**Audience:** One participant, or all participants.

## Template

### Private message

```text
Hi [Participant first name]! 👋 Thanks for jumping in on [Project], it's great to see you contributing.

A quick, friendly heads-up: a maintainer noticed that [your PR / your comment] at [Link] looks like it may have been written with an AI tool, and it didn't include a disclosure. That happens a lot as people get started, so no worries, but it matters to the project.

A few reminders:
- [Project]'s AI policy comes first and overrides anything in our series guidance: [Link]
- If you used AI, say so in the PR description, in the way the policy asks.
- Make sure you understand and can explain every line you submit. Maintainers will ask.
- Reply to reviewers in your own words. Reviewers are volunteering their time and should always get a human-written reply.
- Keep changes small and focused, and remove AI-generated comments that don't add anything.

Could you update the PR with a disclosure [and address any policy points]? If you have questions about what's allowed, ask in [Project channel] or ask me. Thanks! 🙌
```

### Channel reminder

```text
📜 Quick AI reminder for everyone!

AI tools can be great for learning a codebase, finding issues, and checking your own work. But maintainers still have to review everything we send, so:
✅ Read your project's AI policy, it overrides series guidance: [Project]: [Link] · [Project]: [Link]
✅ Disclose AI use the way your project asks
✅ Understand and own every line you submit
✅ Reply to reviewers in your own words
✅ Keep PRs small and focused

Questions? Ask in your project channel. Thanks for being great open source citizens! 🙌
```

## How to use

- Send the private version first and keep it kind. Most cases are honest mistakes.
- Check the specific project's policy before sending. Some projects prohibit AI-written PR descriptions or comments entirely; some reject undisclosed AI contributions outright.
- If a maintainer has already closed the PR, acknowledge that and focus on what to do next time.
- Repeated issues after a reminder go to the Team Lead and Program organizer.

# Waitlist-to-accepted email

Offers a spot to someone from the waitlist, with a shorter confirmation window and the full onboarding checklist.

**When:** Between [Confirm-by date] (T-4w) and [Waitlist decision date] (about T-1w), whenever a spot opens.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with the mini-cohort lead copied.

**Audience:** A waitlisted applicant being offered a spot.

## Template

```text
Subject: 🎉 A spot opened up: you're in the Sustaining Open Source Series!

Hi [Participant first name]!

Great news: a spot has opened up, and you've been selected for the Sustaining Open Source Series! You'll be contributing to [Primary project] as your primary project, with [Secondary project] as your secondary.

Please confirm by [Confirm-by date] with a quick "I'm in!" reply-all. If you can't take the spot, just let us know so we can offer it to the next person.

Your mini-cohort lead is [Team Lead name] (copied here). They'll check in with you weekly and can help with anything: hub access, setup, project questions.

Key dates
- Office hours: [Office hours day and time, with UTC], [Start date] to [End date]. First session: [Start date].
- Zoom: [Zoom link]. Please block the time now.

Your checklist (everyone else has a head start, so please start soon!)
1. Join [Hub workspace name] with your personal email ([Hub invite link]), then message [Team Lead name] so we can add you to [Hub channel]. Introduce yourself there.
2. Set up your environment for both projects: [Setup guide link]. Project guide: [Project guide link].
3. Join each project's community: [Primary project]: [Project channel]. [Secondary project]: [Project channel].
4. Send us your public GitHub username if it wasn't in your application.
5. Read the collaboration guidance: [Collaboration guidance link]
[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: 6. File [Approval process] by [Date]. It routes to your manager for approval.]
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: 7. Track your hours through [Hour tracking process]; they're eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

We're so glad you're joining us, and excited to see what you'll build across [Primary project] and [Secondary project]!

Best,
[Program organizer name]
on behalf of the Sustaining Open Source Series team
```

## How to use

- Offer the spot in the same project as the seat that opened, or use the applicant's own ranking; flag any placement you inferred rather than chose.
- Give a short confirmation window (three to five days) so the next person still has time if they decline.
- If any internal deadline (approval, hour tracking) has already passed for the main cohort, give a new date.
- If you'll be away, name a Team Lead who will mark confirmations, and ask for reply-all.

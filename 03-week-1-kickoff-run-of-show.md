# Week 1 Kickoff Run of Show

The minute-by-minute plan for the first office hours session, plus the participant announcement that goes out before it.

**When:** T0. Post the announcement at least one business day before (T-2d is ideal). Share the run of show with presenters at T-1w.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Run of show: presenters, mentors, Team Leads. Announcement: all participants in [Hub channel].

### Mentor run of show (60 minutes, hard stop)

Biggest risk: project introductions and the primer overrunning. The timekeeper is strict from the first minute.

- **[HH:00] Welcome and series overview (6 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
  - What this is, why it exists, what success looks like.
  - Everyone's journey is different. Contribution includes code, docs, tests, triage, reviews, and showing up in community meetings.
- **[HH:06] Company introductions (7 min).** About 80 seconds each, strictly held.
  - [Company]: [Presenter name]
  - [Company]: [Presenter name]
  - [Foundation]: [Presenter name]
- **[HH:13] Project introductions (20 to 23 min).** About 4 minutes each.
  - Each project covers what it does, who uses it, where a newcomer can contribute, and introduces its own mentors.
  - [Project]: [Mentor name]
  - [Project]: [Mentor name]
  - A mentor roster slide stays up as the backstop.
- **[HH:36] Open source primer (16 min).** Lead: [Mentor name].
  - Finding issues, reading contributing guides, labels, and asking to be assigned.
  - How a pull request works, start to finish.
  - Navigating an unfamiliar codebase.
  - Non-code pathways: triage, docs, reviews, answering questions, community and working group meetings.
  - Async review is the norm, not rejection.
  - Every project has an AI policy, and it overrides series guidance. Full session in Week [N].
- **[HH:52] How we work together and homework (6 min).** Lead: Team Lead.
  - Hub channel, project channels, notes, resources, how to reach your mini-cohort lead and mentors.
  - [If your company requires internal approval to contribute: File [Approval process] by [Date].]
  - [If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: How to log hours in [Matching program name].]
  - Homework and preview of Week 2.
- **[HH:58] Close (2 min).** Lead: Program organizer.

## How to use

- Week 1 is main room only. Five company intros and five project intros leave no room for breakouts or a live Q&A, so route questions to the channel via the chat monitor.
- Send the run of show to presenters a week ahead with their exact time box.
- After the session, Team Leads follow up with no-shows so waitlist backfill can happen early.
- For Slack, use single-asterisk bold so formatting renders on paste.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The structure is universal; approval and hour-logging lines are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** presenters, approval process line, matching program line, emoji choices.

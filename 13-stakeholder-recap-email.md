# Stakeholder Recap Email

A short, warm update to sponsors and internal stakeholders after office hours, focused on who showed up and what's landing.

**When:** Weekly or every two weeks, within 2 business days after a session (T+0 to T+9w).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Executive sponsors, philanthropy or volunteer-program partners, communications, and partner company contacts. Non-engineering readers.

## Template

```text
Subject: Sustaining Open Source Series: Week [N] Recap

Hi team,

[Warm opener, varied each week.] Week [N] is in the books, and [N] participants joined office hours this week, [about N% of the cohort] (source: attendance export, [Date]). [One line on what that consistency says.]

This week we welcomed [Guest full name], [plain-language role, e.g. a maintainer of [Project]], who [what they shared, in one plain sentence]. [Optional: one line on a milestone, e.g. a first contribution accepted by a project, or a new mentor joining.]

So far, participants have made [N] contributions to [Project list], including [N] pull requests accepted by project maintainers (period: [Start date] to [Date]; source: [Activity report]). [Optional horizon line: midpoint survey, celebration event.]

Agenda and notes: [Link]
[If your company counts office hours as volunteer hours: Attendance list for volunteer-hour credit: attached]

Thank you for [specific support, e.g. setting up volunteer-hour tracking]. [Short, genuine, varied each week.]

Best,
[Program organizer name]
```

## How to use

- Lead with this week's attendance, not total cohort size. Consistency week to week is the story.
- Name guests with full name and a plain-language role. Skip breakout details, agenda mechanics, and jargon. Explain "pull request" if the audience needs it.
- Every number carries its period and source. Leave a visible placeholder rather than estimating.
- Keep it to 2 to 3 short paragraphs that fit in one scroll.
- Don't include self-reported survey intent as an outcome here; keep that for internal planning.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. Structure is generic; recipients and the volunteer-hour line are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** recipient list, volunteer-hour attachment line, how pull requests are explained to your audience.

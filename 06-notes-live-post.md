# "Notes Are Live" Post

A two-line celebration post that points participants to the session notes and recording.

**When:** Weekly, within 1 to 2 business days after each session, once notes have been checked against the transcript and chat.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** All participants in [Hub channel].

## Template

```text
@[Participant group] 📝 Week [N] office hours notes are LIVE!!!! Check the link [Link] for a full recap: [highlight 1] [emoji], [highlight 2, with @[Mentor name] if they led it] [emoji], and [highlight 3] [emoji]!! Recording is [Link]. Give it a read and drop questions or follow-ups in the channel! 🎉
```

Example highlight emoji:

- 🔄 setup workarounds and troubleshooting
- 💡 breakout work and reviews
- 🗺️ finding issues
- 🤝 community and collaboration
- 🎉 celebrations and milestones

## How to use

- Post only after the notes have been checked against the transcript and Zoom chat (see the office hours notes template).
- Pick 2 to 3 highlights of 3 to 6 words each. Name the mentor who led the main segment.
- Keep it to one paragraph that fits in 2 to 3 lines in Slack.
- If the recording is restricted to one company's employees, say so, so others aren't confused by an access error.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. Generic participant comms.
- **Company-specific parts:** participant group mention, where recordings live and who can access them.
- **Derived from:** otel-notes-announcement skill.

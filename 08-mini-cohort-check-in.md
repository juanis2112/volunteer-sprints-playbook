# Mini-Cohort Check-In

A weekly 1:1 message from a mini-cohort lead to each person in their group, plus a gentle nudge for someone who has gone quiet.

**When:** Weekly from T+1w. Nudge after 2 weeks with no visible activity or reply (often around the T+4w engagement triage).

**Sent by / owner:** Mini-cohort lead (usually a company mentor or Team Lead).

**Audience:** One participant, by direct message.

## Template

### Weekly check-in

```text
Hi [Participant first name]! 👋 Quick weekly check-in from me as your mini-cohort lead.

How's it going with [Project]? A few things I'd love to hear:
- What are you working on right now? (An issue, a review, docs, reading the codebase, or still exploring all count.)
- Anything blocking you? Setup, finding the right issue, a stalled PR, time?
- Anything I can help with or connect you to?

No need for a long reply, a sentence is great. This is a check-in, not a chase. 🙂
```

### Nudge for someone gone quiet

```text
Hi [Participant first name]! Just checking in, I haven't seen you around [Hub channel] or office hours the last couple of weeks, and I wanted to make sure you're doing OK.

Totally normal for work or life to get busy. If you'd like to jump back in, a few easy on-ramps:
- Drop into office hours on [Office hours day and time, with UTC] and go to any breakout room
- Pick something small from the curated list: [Link]
- Review an open PR or answer a question in [Project channel]

If your situation has changed and you need to step back, just let me know so we can plan around it. Either way, no pressure. 💛
```

## How to use

- Send individually, not as a group ping. Individual messages get far more replies.
- Before sending, check what you already know: attendance, channel activity, any contribution in the project. Mention something specific if you can.
- Log replies and blockers in the cohort tracker so the Team Lead sees patterns (for example, the same issue list running dry).
- If someone confirms they're stepping back, tell the Team Lead promptly so a waitlisted person can be offered the spot while timing still works.

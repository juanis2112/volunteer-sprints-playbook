# Office Hours Notes

The structure for the weekly notes doc, so every session is captured the same way and participants who missed it can catch up.

**When:** Weekly. Draft the same day as the session; publish within 1 to 2 business days.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with a Team Lead or mentor as reviewer.

**Audience:** All participants and mentors. Posted in [Hub channel] or the shared notes doc.

## Template

```text
[Date] | Week [N]: [Session title]

RESOURCES
- Slides: [Link]
- Recording: [Link] ([who can access it])
- Curated issue list: [Link]

ATTENDANCE
- [N] people in the main room (source: Zoom attendance export, [Date])

NOTES
Presented by @[Mentor name] ([Project or Company])

⚡ If you only read one thing
[2 to 3 sentences: the single takeaway]

[Segment 1 title, e.g. 🎤 Overview or demo]
- [Point]
- [Point]

[Segment 2 title, e.g. 🚪 Breakout highlights]
- [Project]: [what came up, blockers cleared, tips]
- [Project]: [what came up]

📈 How we're doing (optional)
- [N] [metric], [period], [source]. Say what the number does not capture, e.g. channel conversations and community meetings.

🎉 Wins
- @[Participant first name] [what they did: opened a PR, gave a first review, joined a community meeting, answered a question]
- [Project] merged [N] contributions this week

✅ Decisions
- [Anything agreed, e.g. a schedule change, a new room format]

💬 From the chat
- [Useful link or question, with who shared it]

📜 AI policy reminder
- Every project's policy comes first. [Project]: [Link]

🔜 Follow-ups
- [Owner]: [action] by [date]
- Unanswered questions posted to [Project channel]: [list]

🧰 Resources
- Hub channel: [Hub channel]
- Project channels: [Project channel], [Project channel]
- Collaboration guidance: [Link]

📝 Homework for Week [N+1]
- [Homework]

👀 Next week: Week [N+1], [Date]
[Topic] · [Office hours day and time, with UTC]
```

## How to use

- **Check AI-generated notes against the raw transcript and the saved Zoom chat before publishing.** AI notes regularly swap figures, misattribute quotes, and miss things said only in chat. Verify every number and every attributed quote.
- Ask before naming anyone in a win if your norms or their company's require it; never post personal details.
- Every number gets its period and source. Don't post ranked leaderboards.
- Keep the "If you only read one thing" section to the real takeaway, not a summary of the agenda.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. Notes structure is company-neutral.
- **Company-specific parts:** where recordings live and access limits, channel names.
- **Derived from:** NumFOCUS Week 4 and Week 7 Office Hours Notes; Office Hours Kit (notes review step).

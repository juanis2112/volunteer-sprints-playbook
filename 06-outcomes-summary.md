# Outcomes Summary

A one-page skeleton for reporting what the series achieved, led by the people who keep showing up, with contributions and hours as supporting evidence.

**When:** First version T+12w (about 2 weeks after the final session). Retention updates at 3 and 6 months after the final session.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Sponsors, partner companies, [Foundation], project maintainers. External-facing versions go through your comms review.

## Template

```text
SUSTAINING OPEN SOURCE SERIES: OUTCOMES SUMMARY
[Partner companies] × [Foundation] · [Project list] · [Start date] to [End date]
Report frozen: [Date] · Data window: [Start date] to [End date]

THE HEADLINE
[One or two sentences about people: how many learned to engage with a project and are still showing up.]

PEOPLE WHO KEEP SHOWING UP
- Completion: [N] of [N] enrolled participants were active in the final week (period: final week, [Date range]; population: enrolled participants at T0; source: [attendance export + activity report]). Define "active": attended office hours, or contributed or participated in a project channel, meeting, or repository that week.
- Retention at 3 months: [N] of [N] participants still engaging with an in-scope project (period: [Date range]; population: [enrolled participants / participants active in final week]; source: [activity report or project check]).
- Retention at 6 months: [N] of [N] participants still engaging (period: [Date range]; population: [same as above]; source: [same as above]).
- Office hours: average [N] attendees per session across [N] sessions (period: [Start date] to [End date]; population: participants only; source: attendance export).

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE: CONTRIBUTIONS
- [N] pull requests opened and [N] accepted by project maintainers (period: [Date range]; population: participants only; source: [activity report], in-scope repositories only).
- [N] issues or PRs with participant comments, reviews, or triage (same period, population, source).
- Community participation: [N] participants joined a project community or working group meeting (source: [project or mentor records]). Leave as [N] if not tracked.

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE: TIME
- [N] volunteer hours logged (period: [Date range]; population: [participants only / participants plus mentors and organizers]; source: [hour-tracking system]).
- [If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: [N] hours matched, reported separately from hours logged; note any per-person cap.]

WHAT PARTICIPANTS AND MAINTAINERS SAID
- "[Quote]" [First name, Company], quoted with permission.
- "[Quote]" [Maintainer first name, Project], quoted with permission.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE NEXT TIME
- [Top change from the retro]
- [Second change]

THANK YOU
[Maintainers, mentors, Team Leads, partner companies, Foundation.]
```

## How to use

- Every number carries its period, population, and source. Freeze a report date and data window, and archive the query or script used so figures can be traced later.
- Self-reported survey intent ("I plan to keep contributing," confidence ratings) stays internal. External versions use verified outcomes: completion, retention, accepted contributions, hours logged.
- Completion and retention are separate metrics. Completion is about the final week; retention is about 3 and 6 months after. Don't merge them.
- Use [N] until you have the real figure. Never estimate to fill a slot, and show only current values, not old and new numbers side by side.
- Keep it to one page. Push detail (per-project breakdowns, charts) to an appendix. No ranked leaderboards.

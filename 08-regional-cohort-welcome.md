# Regional cohort welcome

Two messages for a regional (time-zone) cohort: an update to applicants from that region, and a handoff note to the regional mentors who run selection and support.

**When:** T-5w, when the main cohort's decisions go out. The mentor handoff goes first, the applicant update the same day.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer (applicant update); Program organizer to regional Company mentors (mentor note). Regional mentors then send their own acceptance emails using 01-acceptance-email.md.

**Audience:** Applicants located in [Region]; regional Company mentors.

## Template

### Update to regional applicants

```text
Subject: Update on your Sustaining Open Source Series application

Hi,

A quick update on your application to the Sustaining Open Source Series! Since you're joining us from [Region], your application is being reviewed by our dedicated [Region] mentor team. They'll run a cohort with office hours at a time that works for your time zone, and they'll reach out to you directly very soon with next steps.

Thank you for your patience, and for your interest in contributing to [Project list]. We're excited to see the series grow into a truly global effort!

Best,
The Sustaining Open Source Series team
```

### Note to regional mentors

```text
Subject: Sustaining Open Source Series: [Region] applicant responses

Hi [Mentor name], [Mentor name],

As discussed, here are the application responses for all [Region] applicants: [Restricted response sheet link]

The sheet includes each applicant's full answers. We've also included our own scores from the main selection team ([Score key, e.g. "7-10 strong, 4-6 possible, 1-3 not a fit"]), but you're free to make your own decisions on who joins the [Region] cohort.

A few things to keep in mind:
- Cohort size: please plan for what you can support, roughly [N] participants.
- Office hours: [Regional office hours day and time, with UTC]. Main cohort office hours are [Office hours day and time, with UTC] if anyone can join both.
- Selection guide: [Selection rubric link]
- Email templates (acceptance, waitlist, not selected): [Templates link]
- Please copy me on your outreach to participants so we can keep the cohort records in one place.

For reference:
- Setup guide: [Setup guide link]
- Project guide: [Project guide link]
- Collaboration guidance: [Collaboration guidance link]

Thank you so much for leading this. Tell me if anything would help as you go through it.

Best,
[Program organizer name]
```

## How to use

- Decide region boundaries before applications close (the interest form's region / time zone question makes this easy), and route those applicants out of the main scoring pool.
- Share the response sheet with regional mentors with restricted access; never paste personal data into email.
- Agree in advance who sends acceptance, waitlist, and not-selected emails for the region, and make sure every regional applicant gets a final outcome.
- Add regional participants to [Hub channel] and the main participant tracker so they're counted and included in cohort-wide events.

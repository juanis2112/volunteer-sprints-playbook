# Not selected email

Thanks applicants who weren't selected and points them to other ways to contribute to open source.

**When:** T-5w, same day as acceptance and waitlist emails. [Optional: send after waitlist decisions instead, if you'd rather not announce outcomes in two waves.]

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Applicants not selected (one email, all recipients in BCC).

## Template

```text
Subject: Thank you for applying: ways to stay involved in open source

Hi,

Thank you so much for applying to the Sustaining Open Source Series. We received [N] applications and have mentor capacity for [N] spots this round, so we aren't able to offer you a spot in this cohort. We're truly sorry, and we're grateful you raised your hand.

Please don't let that stop you. Here are ways to keep engaging with open source:
- Contribute to [Project list] on your own. Start with each project's contributing guide and issues labeled "good first issue."
- Join a project's community chat, attend a community meeting, or help answer questions. Contribution is much more than code.
[If your company has a general open source contribution program: - Contribute through [Company program], [Company]'s program for employee open source contributions. Details and how to get started: [Program link]]
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: - Open source volunteer time may be eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

Stay connected
- [Company open source channel]
- [Company open source newsletter or mailing list]

We expect to run more cohorts and would love to see you apply again.

Best,
The Sustaining Open Source Series team
```

## How to use

- Put all recipients in BCC; address the email to yourself.
- Share counts only if you're comfortable; otherwise delete that sentence and keep the capacity point.
- Make sure the general contribution program link explains any approval step people need before contributing on their own.
- Add everyone to the mailing list for future cycles if your company allows it.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. Message is shared; the "other ways to contribute" section is company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** general open source contribution program and its approval step, matching program, internal channels.

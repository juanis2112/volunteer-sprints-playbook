# Mentor Meet-and-Greet Agenda

A 45 to 60 minute kickoff that gets every company mentor and project mentor aligned before participants arrive.

**When:** T-1w (after mentors are confirmed, before the Week 1 session).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with each company's Team Lead.

**Audience:** Company mentors, project mentors, Team Leads, mini-cohort leads.

## Template

Calendar invite description:

```text
Sustaining Open Source Series: Mentor Meet-and-Greet

A chance for all of us to meet before the series kicks off! Mentors from [Partner companies] and maintainers from [Project list] in one room, so we can put faces to names before we're with [N] participants.

When: [Date and time, with UTC]
Zoom: [Zoom link]

Before the call (10 minutes):
- Read the mentor expectations: [Link]
- Skim the curated issue list for your project: [Link]
- Mark your office hours availability for all 10 weeks: [Link]

Questions? Reach out in [Mentor channel] or to [Program organizer name].
```

Agenda (60 minutes):

1. **Welcome and introductions (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Everyone: name, company or project, and one thing you hope participants get out of the series.
2. **The series at a glance (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - [N] projects, [N] partner companies, [N] participants, 10 weeks, [Start date] to [End date].
   - Office hours: [Office hours day and time, with UTC].
   - What success looks like: people who learn to engage with a project and keep showing up. Pull requests are one way, not the only way.
3. **What we're asking of mentors (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Company mentors: first line of support, [N] office hours minimum, [N] hours a week, help with environment setup and channel questions.
   - Project mentors: [N] office hours minimum, curate issues, review contributions, host project breakouts.
   - Mini-cohort leads: a weekly 1:1 check-in with each person in your group. A check-in, not a chase.
   - Decline single weeks you can't make. Don't decline the whole series.
4. **Escalation path (5 min).** Lead: Team Lead.
   - Technical blocker: project channel, then the project mentor for that project.
   - Logistics, access, or time questions: the participant's company Team Lead.
   - Conduct concerns or anything sensitive: [Program organizer name] directly, and the project's code of conduct contact.
   - Someone has gone quiet for 2+ weeks: their mini-cohort lead sends a nudge, then flags the Team Lead.
5. **Contribution pathways check (10 min).** Lead: one project mentor per project.
   - Is the curated issue list ready? Each project confirms [N] or more issues that are labeled, unblocked, and have enough context for a newcomer.
   - Who owns refreshing the list each week? Name a person.
   - Non-code pathways: docs, tests, triage, translation, answering questions, reviewing, community and working group meetings. Each project names at least one.
   - AI policy link confirmed for each project.
6. **Office hours coverage (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Walk the coverage sheet. Every week needs 2 or more mentors and a named host for each project breakout.
   - Fill gaps now, with a backup per project.
   - Session roles for Week 1: host, timekeeper, chat monitor, breakout hosts, attendance capture.
7. **Week 1 run of show (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Who presents what and when, so nobody is surprised on the day.
8. **Where everything lives, and Q&A (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Mentor channel, participant hub channel, project channels, agendas, notes, issue list, collaboration guidance.

## How to use

- Fill in the coverage sheet before the call so the coverage segment is about gaps, not data entry.
- Send the invite with the pre-read at least one week ahead, and record the call for mentors who can't attend.
- Leave the call with three things written down: coverage gaps, issue-list owners, and any project without a ready list.
- Follow up the same day in the mentor channel with the decisions and open items.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. The agenda is generic and every run needs it.
- **Company-specific parts:** escalation contacts, mentor time minimums, internal approval steps mentors need to know about, and [If your company requires internal approval to contribute: a reminder of how participants file [Approval process]].
- **Derived from:** NumFOCUS Mentor Meet & Greet slides; Organizer Playbook Step 4; Juanita's playbook Stage 4 (mentor kickoff).

# Outreach tracker

A simple tracker for where the recruitment announcement goes, who posts it, and when to remind.

**When:** Set up at T-11w; update daily during launch week (T-10w) and weekly until [Application deadline].

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer (each row has its own driver).

**Audience:** The organizing team (Program organizer, Team Leads, Company mentors).

## Template

### Fields (one row per channel)

- **Channel:** name of the chat channel, newsletter, mailing list, or event
- **Channel type:** from the list below
- **Link or contact:** URL, channel handle, or newsletter owner
- **Owner / driver:** who posts or pitches it
- **Approval needed:** yes or no, and from whom (channel moderator, newsletter editor)
- **Date posted:** date the announcement went out
- **Version used:** short, long, or one-liner
- **Reminder date:** when to post the reminder or last call
- **Status:** Not started / In progress / Posted / Reminder sent / Declined
- **Notes:** questions received, engagement, follow-ups, "do not repost" norms

### Channel types to target

- **Open source and developer community channels:** company-wide open source channel, developer experience or engineering community channels
- **Technology-specific channels:** channels for the languages and domains the projects use (for example Python, scientific computing, data science, machine learning)
- **Guilds and communities of practice:** technical guilds, working groups, and their chairs' channel
- **Employee resource groups and employee communities:** groups whose members are underrepresented in open source, and early-career, intern, and new-grad communities
- **Mentoring programs:** existing mentorship or STEM volunteering communities
- **Team and department channels:** organizations whose work depends on the projects
- **Learning and development:** training team channels, course catalogs, and learning newsletters
- **Internal newsletters:** engineering newsletter, company-wide news, manager newsletter
- **Banners and intranet:** internal developer portal banners, intranet news, digital signage
- **Mailing lists:** people who expressed interest in previous cycles, past participants and mentors
- **Events:** existing open source office hours, tech talks with a project maintainer, info sessions (05-info-session-agenda.md)
- **Regional and office channels:** local office or regional channels, especially if you are running a regional cohort

## How to use

- Make one row per channel before launch and assign a driver to each; split newsletters and banners to people who know the editors.
- Ask moderators before posting where a channel has rules, and record the answer.
- Set reminder dates so each channel gets at most one reminder plus a last call.
- In launch week, check form responses daily and note which channels drove questions; record lessons for the next cycle.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. The structure is generic; each company fills in its own channels.
- **Company-specific parts:** actual channel names, newsletter owners, banner process, employee communities.

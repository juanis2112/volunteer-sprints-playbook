# Team Lead Reply Snippets

Paste-ready replies for the requests Team Leads get most often in direct messages.

**When:** T-4w to T+2w, mostly while participants are joining channels.

**Sent by / owner:** Team Lead.

**Audience:** One participant, by direct message.

## Template

### "Please add me to the series channel"

```text
Congratulations [Participant first name]!!! 🎉 We're so excited to have you! 🙌

Adding you to *[Hub channel]* now! 🚀 That's our main cohort channel 📣, where we post announcements and where you can connect with everyone across the series: other participants 👥, mentors 🧑‍🏫, and the projects themselves. Ask questions ❓, share what you learn 💡, help each other out! 🤗

Come say hi 👋 and introduce yourself before we kick off on *[Start date]* 📅! Tell us what you're interested in working on ✨ docs 📖, testing 🧪, triage 🔍, performance ⚡, whatever draws you, so we can connect you with people working on similar things 🤝

Our collaboration guidance 📋 has tips on writing your intro, Slack and Zoom setup 🖥️, and our AI use principles 🤖: [Link]

[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: Last thing 🎫: please file [Approval process] by [Date]. It's required, and it's how we track your participation.]

YAY!! 🎉
```

### Short version

```text
Congratulations [Participant first name]!!! 🎉 Adding you to *[Hub channel]* now! 🚀

Come introduce yourself 👋 before we kick off on *[Start date]* and tell us what you'd like to work on ✨ so we can connect you with others doing similar things 🤝 Collaboration guidance (intro tips, Slack and Zoom setup): [Link]

[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: Don't forget to file [Approval process] 🎫]
```

### "Which project channel should I join?"

```text
Great question! Join the channel for your project: [Project channel]. You're welcome in any project's channel and any breakout room, so feel free to explore. Announcements always go in [Hub channel]. 🙌
```

### "I can't make this week's office hours"

```text
No problem at all! Notes and the recording go up in [Hub channel] within a couple of days, and your project channel is always open for questions. See you next time! 🎉
```

## How to use

- Add the person to the channel before you paste, so the message is true when they read it.
- Swap channel names, dates, and links once per run, then reuse.
- Use single-asterisk bold for Slack so it renders on paste.
- If a person can't access Slack, point them to the fallback channel your series published in its official channels doc.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The welcome is generic; the approval line is company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** approval process line, workspace fallback for people without Slack access.
- **Derived from:** NumFOCUS Team Lead Slack Reply Template.

# Mentor and Organizer Retro Agenda

A 60-minute retrospective where mentors, Team Leads, and organizers decide what to keep, change, and write down for the next run.

**When:** T+11w to T+13w (1 to 4 weeks after the final session), once end-of-series survey results are in.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Company mentors, project mentors, Team Leads, mini-cohort leads, co-organizers.

## Template

Invite description:

```text
Sustaining Open Source Series: Mentor and Organizer Retro

Thank you for everything you gave this series. Let's capture what worked and what to change while it's fresh.

When: [Date and time, with UTC]
Zoom: [Zoom link]
Retro board: [Link]

Before the call (10 minutes): add at least one sticky to each column on the retro board, and skim the survey summary: [Link]
```

Agenda (60 minutes):

1. **Welcome and thank-you (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Ground rules: about the program, not people. Every voice counts.
2. **What we heard from participants (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Survey headlines with respondent count and dates. Top blockers. What they'd tell a friend.
   - Outcome snapshot (labeled with period, population, and source).
3. **What worked (10 min).** Everyone.
   - What should we keep exactly as it is?
4. **What to change (25 min).** Everyone. Work through each area:
   - **Issue curation:** Was the list big enough, fresh enough, sized right? Did the weekly owner model work?
   - **Onboarding and setup:** Did the Setup Sprint and setup guides prevent kickoff errors? What broke on which platforms?
   - **Office hours:** Session shape, breakout time, topics, coverage, regional cohort timing.
   - **Mini-cohorts and engagement:** Did weekly check-ins catch people before they dropped off? Right group size?
   - **Comms and channels:** One hub or too many? What did people miss?
   - **Docs:** What in the playbook, project guide, or templates was wrong, missing, or ignored?
   - **Mentor experience:** Time asked versus time given, review load, what support mentors needed.
5. **Owners and next steps (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
   - Each change gets an owner and a target (playbook update, template fix, decision for next run).
   - Who wants to mentor again? Which alumni could mentor?

Retro board columns:

- Keep
- Change
- Stop
- Try next time
- Questions

## How to use

- Run it after the survey closes so participant data informs the discussion.
- Assign a note-taker who writes changes directly into the playbook and templates within a week. The next organizer depends on this step.
- Invite project mentors separately if a shared session would make maintainers less candid about company-side issues, or vice versa.
- Record decisions in the decisions log.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. Retro format is company-neutral.
- **Company-specific parts:** any internal post-mortem format your company requires.
- **Derived from:** Organizer Playbook Step 11; Juanita's playbook Stage 5 Task 3 and Stage 6 (mentor retro, update the guide).

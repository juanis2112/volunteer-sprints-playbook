# Manager notification email

Lets each accepted participant's manager know what the series is, what it asks of their report, and how they can help.

**When:** T-4w, after the participant confirms (or as soon as any internal approval request reaches the manager).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** The manager of each confirmed participant.

## Template

```text
Subject: [Participant first name] is joining the Sustaining Open Source Series

Hi [Manager first name],

I'm writing to let you know that [Participant first name] has been selected for the Sustaining Open Source Series, a 10-week program running [Start date] to [End date] in which employees contribute to [Project list] with mentorship from the project maintainers and from experienced contributors at [Company] and [Partner companies].

[If participation is volunteer time: This is volunteer time, not a work assignment, and it shouldn't affect [Participant first name]'s priorities on your team.]
[If participation is on work time: [Company] supports participation during work hours, up to [N] hours a week.]

What it involves
[Participant first name] will spend about [N] hours a week contributing to [Primary project] and [Secondary project]. That includes code, documentation, testing, reviews, and taking part in the project communities. There's a one-hour office hours session each [Office hours day and time, with UTC] and a short weekly check-in with their mini-cohort lead, [Team Lead name].

[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: You'll receive (or may already have received) a request through [Approval process] to approve [Participant first name]'s participation. Please review it by [Date]. Your approval is the basis for approving their time, so please let us know if the commitment doesn't work for your team.]

What helps most
- A quick conversation with [Participant first name] about how they'll fit the weekly time around their work.
- Flexibility for office hours on [Office hours day].
- Interest in what they're learning: participants who can share their work with their team tend to stick with it.

What [Company] gets
Participants build skills working with maintainers of widely used open source projects, get to know engineers across [Partner companies], and help sustain tools that [Company] depends on.

[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: Their hours are also eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

If you have questions, reply to this email or reach out to [Program organizer name] at [Contact].

Thank you for supporting [Participant first name]'s involvement.

Best,
[Program organizer name]
[Team or program name]
```

## How to use

- Send only after the participant has confirmed, and tell participants in the acceptance email that their manager will receive this note.
- Choose one of the two time framing lines (volunteer time or work time) and delete the other; match your company's actual policy.
- If your approval process already notifies managers, send this as a companion note rather than a duplicate request.
- Merge per manager; if one manager has several participants, combine them into one email.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. Program description is shared; time policy and approval are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** volunteer vs work time policy, approval process, matching program, what the company gets (adjust to your stated goals).

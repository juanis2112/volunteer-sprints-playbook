# Office Hours Calendar Hold

A recurring calendar invite that holds the weekly office hours slot for mentors, Team Leads, and partner contacts for the full series.

**When:** T-4w (send once the slot and Zoom link are final).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** Company mentors, project mentors, Team Leads, partner company contacts. Participants get a separate invite or event registration.

## Template

Title options:

- Sustaining Open Source Series: Weekly Office Hours
- HOLD: Sustaining Open Source Series Office Hours ([Start date] to [End date])

Description:

```text
Weekly office hours for the Sustaining Open Source Series 🎉

Ten weeks, [N] projects ([Project list]), and [N] companies ([Partner companies]) partnering with [Foundation]. This is the one hour a week where the whole cohort comes together.

Zoom: [Zoom link]

WHEN
[Office hours day and time, with UTC], [Start date] through [End date].
[Time in other participant regions]
Clock changes: [Date range] the session shifts by an hour for some regions. We'll restate the UTC time in every announcement.

FORMAT
Each session runs about an hour:
- 15 to 20 minutes: overview or demo on the week's topic
- 30 to 40 minutes: breakout rooms, usually by project
- 5 minutes: wins, homework, and next week

WHAT WE ASK OF MENTORS
- Join when you can and go to your project's breakout room
- No weekly prep unless you're leading that week's overview. We'll reach out well in advance if so
- If you can't make a week, decline that week only, not the whole series

LINKS
- Project guide (onboarding, mentors, curated issues): [Link]
- Collaboration guidance (channels, Zoom, AI use principles): [Link]
- Series FAQ: [Link]
- Hub channel: [Hub channel]. Ask a Team Lead to be added.

PLEASE FORWARD
If someone on your side should hold this time for the full series, forward this invite. We'd rather over-invite than have a mentor miss their project's breakout.

Questions? Reach out to [Program organizer name].
```

## How to use

- Anchor the recurrence to the time zone where most participants sit, and list the UTC time so other regions can convert.
- Check clock-change dates for every region in the cohort. US and EU daylight saving shifts happen on different dates in spring and autumn.
- Decide whether regional cohort mentors (for example a time-zone cohort) should get this hold or a separate regional session instead.
- Replace every bracket and test the Zoom link before sending.

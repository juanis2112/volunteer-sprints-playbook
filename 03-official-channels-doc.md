# Official channels doc

The single "where to talk" reference listing every series channel, who it is for, and what to do if you cannot access it.

**When:** Decide the hub by T-10w; publish by T-4w, before onboarding.
**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer
**Audience:** Participants, mentors, Team Leads

## How to use

- Pick one hub before anything else. Channel sprawl across many workspaces and tools is the most common operational pain.
- Use a private announcement channel plus per-project channels. One channel with hundreds of people is impossible to follow.
- A program organizer or Team Lead must be a member of every private series channel.
- Decide fallbacks before launch: some participants cannot install certain tools, some workspaces require a personal email, and some company networks block content from certain platforms.
- Use bulleted entries (not a table) so the doc is readable when pasted into chat or email.

## Notes for alignment

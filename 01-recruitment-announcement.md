# Recruitment announcement

Announces that applications are open, in a short chat version and a longer email or newsletter version.

**When:** T-10w (applications open), with a reminder post at T-7w and a last call 2-3 days before [Application deadline].

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer, with Team Leads and Company mentors reposting in their own channels.

**Audience:** Prospective participants and mentors at [Company]: engineering chat channels, employee communities, internal newsletters, and the mailing list of people who expressed interest in past cycles.

## Template

### Short version (chat channels)

```text
🚀 The Sustaining Open Source Series is open for applications! 🚀

Spend 10 weeks helping sustain [Project list], with mentorship from the project maintainers and from colleagues at [Partner companies].

💡 Learn and grow: work with maintainers on real code, docs, reviews, and issues
🧠 Get mentorship: weekly office hours plus a small mini-cohort with a lead who checks in with you each week
🤝 Join the community: meet participants from [Partner companies] and the projects themselves
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: 🌱 Your hours are eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

⏱️ About [N] hours/week | 🌍 Remote and flexible | 📅 [Start date] to [End date]

👉 Apply: [Form link]
🗓️ Info session: [Info session date and time]
⏰ Deadline: [Application deadline]

Know the projects well? We are also looking for mentors. Reach out to [Program organizer name].

No prior experience with these projects is required. Please share with anyone who might be interested!
```

### Long version (email, mailing list, or internal newsletter)

```text
Subject: Apply now: Sustaining Open Source Series, [Start date] to [End date]

[If sending to a mailing list: You're receiving this because you previously expressed interest in our open source programs.]

Hi all,

[Company], in collaboration with [Foundation] and [Partner companies], is running the next Sustaining Open Source Series, a 10-week program supporting [Project list].

What it is
Over 10 weeks, you'll be matched to a primary and a secondary project and get hands-on mentorship from project maintainers and from experienced contributors across [Partner companies]. You'll work alongside a cohort of peers, from first-time contributors to open source regulars.

Why join
- Learn and grow: work side by side with maintainers on code, documentation, testing, reviews, and issue triage.
- Get mentorship: weekly office hours, plus a mini-cohort lead who checks in with you one-on-one each week.
- Collaborate: build relationships with participants and maintainers across companies and the wider project community.
- Solve real problems: help improve tools that [Brief description of why the projects matter].
- Make a lasting impact: build skills and an open source track record, and learn how to keep showing up in a community after the series ends.
- Support [Foundation], the nonprofit home of these projects.
[If your company has a volunteer-hour matching program: - Give back twice: volunteer hours are eligible for matching through [Matching program name].]

The details
- Dates: [Start date] to [End date]
- Time: about [N] hours a week, remote and flexible
- Office hours: [Office hours day and time, with UTC]
- No prior experience with these projects is needed. [Language or skill requirement, e.g. "Familiarity with Python is the only requirement."]

How to apply
- Apply here (about [N] minutes): [Form link]
- Learn more: [Program page or FAQ link]
- Come to the info session: [Info session date and time], [Zoom link]
- Deadline: [Application deadline]
[If your company requires internal approval to contribute: Please let your manager know you're applying. Accepted participants will file [Approval process] before the series starts.]

We're also looking for mentors
If you know one or more of these projects well and can give about [N] hours a week to help participants get started, please reach out to [Program organizer name] at [Contact].

[Optional: Join our mailing list for future open source opportunities: [Mailing list link]]

Please share with colleagues who might be interested.

Best,
[Program organizer name]
[Team or program name]
```

### One-liner (banners, digests, signage)

```text
Apply now for the Sustaining Open Source Series ([Start date] to [End date]): 10 weeks of mentored contribution to [Project list] with [Partner companies]. [Form link]
```

## How to use

- Fill in partners, projects, dates, office hours time (with UTC), deadline, and all links, then click every link before posting.
- Check each channel's posting norms and get a moderator's OK where needed; use the short version where long posts are discouraged. Keep both calls to action (apply, and mentor interest) in every version.
- Delete the matching and approval lines if they do not apply at your company.
- Log every post in the outreach tracker (02-outreach-tracker.md) and set a reminder date.
- For newsletter owners, send the long version with a two-line pitch and the one-liner for digests.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The program description is shared across companies; matching, approval, and channel specifics differ.
- **Company-specific parts:** matching program name, internal approval step, mailing list, program page link, chat channel names, organizer contact.
- **Derived from:** Juanita's playbook (Stage 2, Task 1, items 3 and 6: Slack and email message, newsletter announcement); "[Advertisement] NumFOCUS Sustaining Open Source Initiative" (chat short and long versions, newsletter announcement, one-liner, banner, mailing list announcement).

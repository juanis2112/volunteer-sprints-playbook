# Celebration Session Run of Show

The plan for the final office hours: participant stories, mentor reflections, and what comes next. Main room only.

**When:** T+9w (the final session). Recruit storytellers at T+7w; confirm at T+8w.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer (host).

**Audience:** Run of show: mentors and storytellers. Announcement: all participants.

## Template

### Storyteller recruitment (from each mentor)

```text
Hi [Participant first name]! 🎉 Our final session on [Date] is a celebration, and I'd love for you to share your story from the series. About 5 minutes, no slides needed (a screenshot or link is welcome).

A win, a challenge, something you learned, or a moment that surprised you all count. It doesn't have to be a merged PR: a first review, a community meeting you joined, or getting unstuck are great stories too.

Would you be up for it? Let me know by [Confirm-by date]. 🙌
```

### Run of show (60 minutes, no breakouts)

- **[HH:00] Welcome and thank-you (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
  - What this cohort did together, in one or two lines. Any numbers carry period and source.
  - Shared notes doc is open for anyone who'd rather share in writing: [Link]
- **[HH:05] Participant stories (30 min).** Lead: Host, with each mentor introducing their storyteller.
  - One participant per mentor (or per project), 5 minutes each. Up to [N] stories.
  - [Mentor name] introduces [Participant first name] ([Project])
  - [Mentor name] introduces [Participant first name] ([Project])
  - Timekeeper gives a 1-minute warning.
- **[HH:35] Mentor reflections (10 min).** Lead: one mentor per project and company, about 1 minute each.
  - What surprised you? What will you remember?
- **[HH:45] What's next (10 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
  - Keep contributing: project community meetings, channels, triage, review, and the path to roles like triager, reviewer, and maintainer.
  - [If your company has a program for continued contribution: how to keep going through [Company program].]
  - End-of-series survey link: [Form link]
  - Mentoring next time: [Form link]
- **[HH:55] Thank-yous and close (5 min).** Lead: Program organizer.
  - Thank maintainers, mentors, Team Leads, partner companies, [Foundation], and everyone who showed up.

Roles during the session:

- Host: [Name]
- Timekeeper: [Name]
- Chat monitor (captures shout-outs and stories shared in chat for the notes): [Name]
- Attendance capture: [Name]

Open items before [Date]:

- [ ] One storyteller confirmed per mentor or project
- [ ] Running notes doc created and linked
- [ ] Consent to record and to quote stories in follow-up materials
- [ ] [If holding one: optional in-person celebration details]

## How to use

- Ask mentors to recruit storytellers who reflect different journeys, not only the people with the most merged PRs.
- Get consent before recording stories or using them in blogs or reports.
- Keep the shared notes doc open so people who are shy about the mic can still share.
- Post the notes and recording within a week, with the survey link again.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public core + company add-on. The session is generic; continued-contribution programs are company-specific.
- **Company-specific parts:** continuation programs, any in-person celebration event, consent wording.

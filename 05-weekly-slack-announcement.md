# Weekly Slack Announcement

A short, high-energy post that tells participants what's happening at this week's office hours and what to do before and after.

**When:** Weekly, 2 days before each session (T+1w to T+9w).

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer or Team Lead.

**Audience:** All participants and mentors in [Hub channel].

## Template

```text
@here WEEK [N] IS [DAY]!!! 🎉🚀
[Session title] [Project emoji run]

📅 [Day, Month DD]
🕚 [Office hours day and time, with UTC]
💻 Zoom 👉 [Zoom link]

[Optional clock-change line, e.g. ⏰ [Region]: your clocks changed this weekend, so we're an hour EARLIER for you this week only!]

NEW this week: [one line on what's special, e.g. a live demo from @[Mentor name]] 🎥

What we'll cover 📝
[emoji] [item]
[emoji] [item]
[emoji] [item]

Breakout rooms 🚪 [emoji] [Project] · [emoji] [Project] · [emoji] [Project]

Before [Day] ✅
[emoji] [prep item, e.g. read your project's AI policy 👉 [Link]]

Homework for Week [N+1] 📚 [one or two short bullets that build on last week's homework]

Week [N-1] notes are up in the channel if you missed them 📝

See you [Day]!! 🎉
```

## How to use

- Keep it short: a 3-line header, a bulleted "What we'll cover," rooms on one line, 1 to 2 homework bullets. No paragraphs.
- Always restate the time with UTC. Recheck it the week of any clock change, and don't copy last week's line blindly.
- Show the full Zoom link rather than a "HERE" hyperlink, which can break on paste.
- Homework is always for the following week, and should mix code and non-code options (review a PR, join a community meeting, answer a question in a project channel).
- Sentence case, contractions, second person. Capitalize a word or two for emphasis, not whole sentences. Pick a signature closing emoji and use it every week.

## Notes for alignment

- **Recommendation:** Public. Purely participant-facing logistics.
- **Company-specific parts:** channel names, meeting link, emoji per project, house sign-off.
- **Derived from:** NumFOCUS Week 4 and Week 7 Slack Announcements; numfocus-office-hours skill (announcement style rules).

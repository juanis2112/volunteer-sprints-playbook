# End-of-Series Survey

A participant survey that captures where people landed, what helped, what to fix, and what they plan to do next.

**When:** Opens the week after the final session (T+10w). Stays open 2 to 3 weeks with one reminder.

**Sent by / owner:** Program organizer.

**Audience:** All participants, including those who stepped back partway.

## Template

Intro text:

```text
🎉 You did it! Let's reflect.

Thank you for being part of the Sustaining Open Source Series. This 5 to 7 minute survey helps us improve the next run. Answers are used only by the organizers. If you stepped back partway through, we especially want to hear from you.
```

Questions:

1. **Which project(s) did you contribute to?** (multiple choice: [Project list])
2. **⭐ How would you rate your overall experience?** (1 to 5 stars)
3. **🏁 Where did you land?** (check all that apply)
   - Had a contribution merged 🎯
   - PR submitted, waiting on review 👀
   - Started something, still in progress 🛠️
   - Reviewed PRs, triaged issues, or answered questions 🔍
   - Became a regular in project channels or community meetings 🤝
   - Explored and learned a lot, no contribution yet 🗺️
   - Didn't get to it 😅
4. **🚧 What got in your way?** (check all that apply)
   - ⏰ Finding the time
   - 🔍 Finding the right issue
   - 🧠 Understanding the codebase
   - 🔁 The PR or review process
   - 🛠️ Dev environment setup
   - 🤷 Nothing, it went smoothly!
5. **💡 Biggest "aha" moment or favorite memory from the series?** (open text)
6. **🎒 What are you taking with you?** (check all that apply)
   - 🧭 I know how to navigate an open source project now
   - 🤝 Connections with the project's maintainers
   - 💪 Confidence I didn't have 10 weeks ago
   - 🏠 A community I belong to
   - 📦 A contribution I'm proud of
   - 🔥 Momentum I don't want to lose
7. **Which parts of the series were most useful?** (check all that apply: overview sessions, breakout rooms, mini-cohort check-ins, project channels, curated issue list, setup docs, notes and recordings)
8. **🔮 What's next for you?**
   - Already planning my next contribution 🚀
   - Want to keep going, just need time ⏳
   - Interested, but need help finding what's next 🗺️
   - Interested in mentoring in a future series 🧑‍🏫
   - Taking a break for now 😴
9. **🙌 One thing we nailed:** (open text)
10. **🔧 One thing we should fix:** (open text)
11. **📣 What would you tell someone considering the next series?** (open text)
12. **May we quote your answers (first name and company only) in series materials?** (Yes / Yes, anonymously / No)
13. **💬 Anything else, for us or the mentors?** (open text)

## How to use

- Send it within a week of the final session while memories are fresh. Send one reminder before it closes.
- Self-reported answers, especially intent ("what's next"), stay internal. External reports use verified outcomes instead.
- Report every result with the respondent count, the cohort size it came from, and the survey dates.
- Only quote people who said yes to question 12, in the form they agreed to.
- Pull the "interested in mentoring" list into next run's mentor recruitment.
